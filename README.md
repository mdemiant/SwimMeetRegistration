# SwimMeetRegistration

Swimming competitions are events where swimmers compete in different strokes and distances, such as freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly. Before a competition, swimmers must register for the events they want to participate in and provide their times.

After registration, swimmers are usually organized into heats according to their registered times. Faster swimmers are placed in the central lanes, while slower swimmers are placed toward the outside lanes. Each event is scheduled on a specific day or time, allowing the competition to run in an organized way.
Swim Meet Registration is a program that simulates the registration and organization process for swimmers participating in a swimming competition. It collects information such as the swimmer’s name, age, selected events, and times. Based on this information, the program determines the swimmer’s competition category and the day they will compete in each event.

The program will also simulate how swimmers are organized into heats and lanes. Swimmers will be ordered according to their registered times, with the fastest swimmers placed toward the center lanes and slower swimmers toward the outside lanes. This will allow the program to generate a simplified competition schedule similar to a real swimming meet sheet.

This project is interesting because a swimming competition involves more than simply registering swimmers. Their information must be collected, processed, and organized so that the competition can be conducted correctly. The project will help demonstrate how algorithms can be used to solve and automate part of this process.

The main purpose of the project is to learn and understand a real-world process through algorithms and programming, while applying Python concepts such as variables, conditional statements, loops, lists, sorting, and functions.

Algorithm
1. Start the program.
2. Display the competition information and available swimming events.
3. Ask how many swimmers will be registered.
4. For each swimmer:
-Enter their name.
-Enter their age.
5. Determine their competition category based on their age.
6. Display the available events.
7. Select the events in which they will participate.
8. Enter their time for each selected event.
9. Save the swimmer's information.
10. Organize the registered swimmers according to their selected events.
For each event:
11. Sort the swimmers according to their registered times, from fastest to slowest.
12. Divide the swimmers into heats if necessary.
13. Assign lanes according to their times.
14. Place the fastest swimmers toward the center lanes and slower swimmers toward the outside lanes.
15.Generate the swimmer's registration showing:
-Name
-Age
-Category
-Selected events
-Registered times
-Competition days
16.Generate a simplified competition sheet showing the event, heat, lane, swimmer, and registered time.
-Display the general final competition schedule where all of the swimmers are showed in it.
End the program.
